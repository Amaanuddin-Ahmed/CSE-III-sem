## How to use the report

- `severity=error` rows are blocking: fix before the rebuilt deck ships.
- `severity=warn` rows need a human decision; many are legacy formatting, not wrong content.
- `info` rows are observations (NOT questions, missing Keywords line, flagged extensions).
- The `issues` column is semicolon-separated, with the severity repeated per issue.
- Duplicate text is reported on the later ID only.

## Known limits

- The emitted banks have no `topicId` (schema unchanged), so this audit cannot prove a card matches its unit; it only proves the card is internally valid and not obviously out of scope. Topic coverage is tracked in `coverage_matrix.csv` from the canonical `topicId` sidecar plus keyword matching.
- Flashcard kind (Concept/Formula/Trap/Procedure/Compare/Answer-skeleton) is inferred from topic and prompt because the schema has no field for it.
- Word counts strip LaTeX but keep visible symbols, so a formula-heavy card can be one or two words above a limit without being unreadable.
